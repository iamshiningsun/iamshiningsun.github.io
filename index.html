About me

Who I am

A creative developer who treats every project like a final round — full send, no holding back. I live in the space where aesthetics meet engineering, building interfaces that are as fun to use as they are to look at.

When I'm not pushing pixels or wrangling APIs, I'm probably deep in Valorant, drawing UX inspiration from the most meticulously designed FPS on the planet. Clove is my main. Obviously.

I believe in shipping fast, iterating faster, and never, ever settling for "good enough" when "exceptional" is right there.

Now playing: future nostalgia for immortal creatures — the soundtrack of every late-night build session.
